Drausible shows your Plausible Analytics stats on your phone. Add a server, whether that is
plausible.io or a Community Edition instance you run yourself, add the sites you want to watch, and
you get the dashboard without opening a browser.

It talks to the servers you configure and to nothing else. There is no account to make, and nothing
about how you use it goes anywhere.

What you get per site:

* Visitors, visits, pageviews, views per visit, bounce rate and average visit duration
* Visitors over time as a chart, bucketed by the hour when you look at a single day
* Ranges from one day out to twelve months, or any pair of dates you pick
* Top pages, sources and countries, plus devices with a switch between device type, browser and OS
* How many people are on the site right now, kept up to date while the screen is open

A few things that matter if you self-host:

* Servers too old for the v2 stats API fall back to v1 automatically
* Plain HTTP works, since instances on a home network often run without TLS
* Each server can go through its own SOCKS5 proxy, so a .onion address works with Orbot

You need a stats API key from your Plausible account. You add sites by their site ID, since listing
them needs an API that Community Edition does not have.
